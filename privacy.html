Privacy

Snap-on Toolbox Tracker · Last updated October 11, 2026

What this site stores

  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address from Google, so the site knows which toolbox is yours.
  • Your toolbox: the tools and wishlist items you add, including part numbers, prices you enter, notes, and where you keep them.
  • Your photos: any photos you upload of your tools.
  • Store prices you add: when you add or update a store price, we keep a record of which account added it and when, so a wrong price can be traced and undone.

If you use the site without signing in, your toolbox stays in your own browser and nothing about it is saved by us. The site does send the part numbers you track to look up shared store prices, and your browser loads product photos from Snap-on's image server by part number.

Who can see it

Only you can see your toolbox. Other people can see the shared store prices and store photos for part numbers, but not who owns which tools or who added a price. Photos you upload are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses so they can show up on your cards.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Supabase, the database service this site uses. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than running your tracker.

Store prices and photos

Store prices are added by the people who track each tool, after checking Snap-on's online store, so they can be out of date. Product photos load from Snap-on's public image server. This site isn't affiliated with Snap-on.

Deleting your data

You can delete any tool or photo yourself on the site. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email iesofad@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.
