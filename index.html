Truck account

Buying off the truck? Truck accounts get paid off in 8 to 10 weeks. Put in what you owe, how often you pay and the interest, and BoxTally works out your payment.

Tool insurance

Keep your policy from your own insurer here, and see if it covers what your box is worth. BoxTally doesn't sell insurance. Only you can see this, and it's never on your share link.

Insurers usually want a list of what you own and what it's worth.

Share my box

A read-only link anyone can open, no sign-in needed. It shows your tools as drawings with names and part numbers. It never shows your photos, notes, where things are kept, who has them, or your name and email.

Delete my account

This permanently deletes everything BoxTally keeps for your account: every tool in your box and wishlist, your photos, your share link and your price-drop alerts. It can't be undone.
